# Welcome, weary internet traveler

This repo is meant to serve as a little corner of the internet for me to
show off some of the projects I am working on, as well as some of the fun
things that I get up to. My goal is to use this as a way for you to get
to know me, what I like, and how I work.

## History

This site has been up for quite a few years now, and it went through many iterations.
I started learning web dev in 2021, while I was working as a music teacher.
When I decided I wanted to be a developer full time, I started building this out
as a way to show my work when I applied for jobs. I built the app out in NextJS.

I went years without touching the site once I got a job, but I came back to it when
I started really thinking about how I want to have an internet home that is not just
on social media. I want to show off some projects, do fun things, and just show
what I am like as a person.

When I came back in April 2026, I decided to rewrite the application in Astro. Astro
has had major success in the years since I started this project, and it just made
less sense to use NextJS for what is a mostly static website. I migrated fully over
to Astro, and then redesigned the site.
